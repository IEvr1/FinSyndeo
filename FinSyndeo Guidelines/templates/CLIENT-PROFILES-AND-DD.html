FinSyndeo client profiles and internal due diligence report

Review samples dated 6 October 2026. All names, references and amounts below are fictional. These files demonstrate the proposed format; they are not screened client files and must not be sent to an institution as a real introduction.

Output rules common to every route

Use the heading FinSyndeo Client Profile for the external introduction attachment. Do not call it a completed or certified KYC report. Show case reference, applicant-confirmed assessment revision, generated date, requested service and an information-source legend. Values are Applicant declared, Official source checked, or Not provided / not verified. Keep facts and source-supported information separate from model suggestions. Do not fill missing fields with guessed or convenient defaults.

No document upload is part of the current applicant intake. Accordingly, do not say documents received, identity verified, audited accounts reviewed or document completeness unless a future phase actually collects and verifies them. Asking whether accounts exist is not reviewing accounts. Provider policy documents and introducer invoices are separate internal document flows; this rule does not remove those.

All relevant people and entities have structured identities, roles and linked evidence in the internal subject ledger. The external profile includes only necessary applicant-authorised factual information. Internal risk conclusions, suspicious-activity material, unresolved allegations, internal comments and provider acceptance policies remain restricted to SOPHIMA. The institution receives only the selected profile revision after the approved sharing workflow.

Formatting proposal: logo, case metadata, one-page executive profile where feasible, short route-specific tables, a factual ownership diagram where relevant and a clear footer stating preliminary information. Expanded information goes in an appendix; internal evidence does not become an attachment automatically.

Proposed internal report format for every prospect

Title: SOPHIMA Internal Pre-Introduction Assessment

Confidential. Never automatically shared with institutions, introducers or counterparties.

  1. Case reference, applicant, route, assessment revision, report version, generated time and as-of time.
  2. Status: incomplete / checks pending / clarification / potential match hold / policy block / legally restricted / approved for a specified introduction. Document the distinction.
  3. Information completeness and authority. Exact information provided, missing, self-declared, public-source checked or unverified. No invented document-completeness score.
  4. Applicant and registry review. Legal identity, registration/jurisdiction, status, source, date and discrepancy.
  5. Applicant ownership/control tree. Persons/entities, direct and indirect holdings, roles, jurisdictions, source and verification state. Separate roles such as director and owner.
  6. Related-entity and UBO-interest map. Include disclosed or evidence-supported affiliates, group entities and other interests; unknown links/percentages remain unknown.
  7. Explicit subject ledger and screening results.
  8. Country exposure and rule analysis on separate axes.
  9. Declared activity versus website observations with sources and inconsistencies.
  10. Licence/registration holder, authority, scope, jurisdiction and current validity.
  11. Funds/wealth or processing/financing findings relevant to the route.
  12. Selected-provider fit with essential conditions, preferences and unknowns.
  13. Limitations, clarification tasks, mitigations and reasoned recommendation.
  14. Actual decision authority, conditions, recorded evidence, expiry/re-screen trigger and release state.

Every screened subject must be identifiable in the report. A group brand name is not automatically the name of a legal entity. Individuals are screened for PEP exposure; entities are assessed for relevant PEP connections through identified people. Absence of a list hit is not a verified clean identity. An unresolved possible match is not a proven sanction.

The ledger covers relevant disclosed or supported parties necessary for the proposed introduction, with a recorded inclusion reason. It is not a promise to discover every affiliate worldwide or screen every retail customer. Add a person/entity only for a justified purpose. Under SOPHIMA's introducer-only design premise, do not describe this as statutory bank AML KYC or a suspicious-activity-reporting process. Criminal-offence media processing remains disabled until an applicable legal route and safeguards are established; the report can state Not enabled for that check. Internal confidentiality does not remove applicable privacy rights.

The future crawler is bounded, secured against SSRF and prompt injection, and treats fetched text as untrusted evidence. It does not follow arbitrary internal links, execute instructions or decide licence validity by website wording alone.

Diagram format

Applicant structure: Person A → 60% ownership → Holding entity → 100% ownership → Applicant; Person B → 40% ownership → Holding entity. Each link includes jurisdiction and source.

Related interests: Person A → evidenced directorship → Entity X. Do not label that directorship as ownership. No attempt to discover every company worldwide is promised. Report coverage and limitations.

Release conditions

A report is created for every submitted prospect, including incomplete or blocked cases. It can say not run. A profile is not introduction-ready while mandatory checks are stale, unavailable, incomplete or unresolved. The authorised external profile and introduction draft reference the same case revision, institution and sharing approval. Material changes invalidate readiness. Retention follows the approved purpose-specific policy; account deletion does not simply wipe accounting history or keep a full dossier forever.

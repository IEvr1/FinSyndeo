FinSyndeo / SOPHIMA — Introducer Agreement Review Drafts

Prepared 6 October 2026 for Marios's review and later completion by the appropriate legal and accounting advisers. Not ready for execution. This is a proposed commercial structure derived from the supplied business rules. Bracketed particulars and decision points must be completed; signatures must not be requested against this draft.

Use one Master Agreement with either the Standard Automatic Tier Schedule or the Bespoke Commercial Schedule. The manual Admin control is an internal implementation tool; it does not by itself amend agreed contractual terms. No legal conclusion about enforceability, regulatory permissions, VAT or retention periods is expressed by this draft.

A. Master Introducer Agreement — proposed review text

Agreement particulars

SOPHIMA: [full legal contracting entity], registered in [jurisdiction] under number [number], with registered address [address] (“SOPHIMA”), operating the FinSyndeo platform.

Introducer: [full legal name / registered company name], [individual or legal entity], [country], [company registration number where applicable], [address], [approved account email], [VAT/tax identification where applicable] (“Introducer”).

Authorised signatories: [SOPHIMA name and capacity] and [Introducer name and capacity / authority].

Effective date: [date and time, including time zone]. Agreement version: [version]. Applicable Schedule 1: [standard/bespoke version].

1. Appointment and permitted role

SOPHIMA appoints the Introducer on a [non-exclusive / other agreed basis] to refer prospective applicants through FinSyndeo for the facilities within the agreed referral scope. The Introducer is an independent contracting party and has no authority to bind SOPHIMA, a provider or an applicant, to promise approval or account opening, or to collect or hold client funds on their behalf.

The Introducer must not present the referral service as regulated financial advice, an assurance of suitability or guaranteed onboarding. Any activity requiring separate authorisation may be undertaken only to the extent the party holds the applicable authorisation. The permitted countries, marketing practices and referral scope are [to be agreed].

2. Invitation-only access and referral attribution

SOPHIMA creates and approves the Introducer's account. The Introducer must use the approved sign-in method, protect account access and promptly report suspected compromise.

The Introducer receives one unique referral link. The link is for attribution only and is not a login credential. Referrals must originate through that link or another specifically documented method approved by SOPHIMA. A valid referral is linked to the canonical applicant/client, application, provider relationships and commission records.

An existing client, competing attribution, duplicate submission or disputed authority is held for resolution under the documented referral rules. No client is transferred between introducers merely because a later link was used. Attribution corrections require supporting evidence, a documented decision and an audit record; legitimate historical records remain preserved subject to the approved retention policy.

3. Applicant authority and confirmation

An applicant may complete their own questionnaire. If the Introducer completes it for an applicant, the Introducer must be authorised and accept the following declaration for each application:

I confirm that I have been authorised by the applicant to provide this information and submit this application on their behalf, and that evidence of such authority can be provided to FinSyndeo/SOPHIMA upon request.

The Introducer must provide accurate information to the best of their knowledge and must not invent or conceal applicant details. Evidence of representative authority must be available on request. Applicant review and confirmation of the relevant application revision, applicable terms and privacy information are required before a provider introduction proceeds. Separate approval of the selected provider and external sharing package is recorded through the applicant's verified account or another approved evidence process.

4. Provider participation and discretion

Only providers that meet SOPHIMA's approved listing, referral-agreement, product eligibility, regulatory and reporting requirements may participate. For introducer-originated applications, providers must supply analytical client-level commission reporting meeting the agreed requirements. A provider unable to supply that reporting is excluded from the introducer pathway; this does not by itself exclude an otherwise approved provider from a separate direct-applicant pathway.

SOPHIMA may pause a provider or referral pathway when required by the governing policy, missing evidence, service unavailability or applicable obligations. Providers make their own onboarding and facility decisions. Matching, an introduction and pre-approval do not constitute account activation or a guarantee that the facility will be provided.

5. Confidentiality and provider identity

Each party must protect confidential information obtained through the relationship and use it only for the agreed purposes. This includes applicant data, provider acceptance policies, commercial terms and documents, commission information and internal reports. Disclosure is limited to authorised recipients with a legitimate need and any disclosure required by applicable obligations.

Introducer-facing records identify providers by FinSyndeo code. The Introducer must not seek to defeat that confidentiality restriction or redistribute provider policies and confidential materials. An applicant may receive the provider identity at the approved introduction/onboarding stage. Any exceptional disclosure to the Introducer must be expressly authorised.

The non-circumvention commitment applies to [precisely identified relationships/information], for [agreed duration], subject to [existing relationships, independent sources, lawful disclosures and other reviewed exceptions]. This paragraph must be completed and reviewed; it must not create an unlimited restraint on legitimate business.

6. Data protection, permitted access and retention

Each party must handle personal information according to the agreed documented purposes, notices, security and retention requirements. The parties' actual roles as controller, joint controller or processor for each processing activity will be determined and recorded in [applicable data protection schedule / agreement]; no universal role is assumed here.

The Introducer may access only information necessary for their own authorised referrals, status history, financial statements and documents. Internal SOPHIMA screening findings, unrelated third-party records, confidential provider policies and internal commercial approval reasons are not provided to the Introducer.

Requests to correct, access or delete information must follow the approved rights-handling process. Closing an account or deleting an applicant profile does not silently erase financial obligations or evidence lawfully retained under the approved schedule. Personal information that is no longer required will be deleted or appropriately de-identified through that schedule. Neither party may retain all information indefinitely merely because attribution is historical. Security incidents and data-subject requests must be reported through [contacts and procedure].

7. Commission entitlement and Schedule 1

The Introducer's commission entitlement is determined by Schedule 1 and is calculated only on eligible Net Provider Commission actually received by SOPHIMA for their attributed client relationships. An applicant's separate platform/introduction fee is not included unless expressly stated in an executed Schedule.

Eligible Net Provider Commission is the provider remuneration cash actually received less any cash VAT/indirect tax included in that receipt, subject to an expressly authorised accounting adjustment. Withholding tax and agreed, directly attributable bank/intermediary charges are deducted once when reconciling the stated gross commission to the cash received. If the actual receipt is already net of those deductions, they are not deducted again from that receipt. These components, currency and evidence are recorded separately. Any uncertain tax, cash or contractual treatment is held for review; an AI extraction or projected provider statement is not evidence that cash has settled.

For reconciliation, let G be stated gross provider commission, V the separately recorded cash VAT, W withholding tax and B directly attributable bank/intermediary charges. If VAT is included in G, expected cash is G − W − B and eligible net is expected cash − V. If VAT is added to G, expected cash is G + V − W − B and eligible net is expected cash − V = G − W − B. With no cash VAT, expected cash and eligible net are G − W − B. Actual cash must match expected cash before automatic posting; a discrepancy requires an approved allocation/adjustment rather than a second deduction.

Entitlement timestamp decision: the current prototype applies the rate at final approved ledger posting, with receipt date and activity period recorded separately. Approved prototype rule C01: retain final approved ledger posting; no retrospective recalculation. No historical posted entry is retrospectively recalculated when a later tier/rate applies.

8. Statements and portal invoicing

SOPHIMA makes separate commission receipt/adjustment entries available through FinSyndeo. Each entry records the client, provider code, period, currency, approved net provider commission, applicable rate and Introducer share.

The Introducer may consolidate one or more eligible entries, including an agreed partial allocation, into one invoice in a single currency. An allocation reserved against an invoice cannot be invoiced again. The invoice subtotal must equal the selected commission allocations; invoice VAT/indirect tax is stated separately according to the reviewed applicable treatment.

Invoices must be uploaded through FinSyndeo and linked to the eligible entries. Ordinary email does not create an operational invoice or payment request. A rejected invoice remains in history and a corrected version is linked to it. Statement/invoice disputes must be raised through [procedure/contact] within [agreed period], without limiting rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

9. Payment and reconciliation

Payment is subject to a valid approved invoice, actual provider receipt, valid payment particulars, applicable verification and the absence of an unresolved hold. The agreed payment period is [number of days / trigger], in [currency and method]. SOPHIMA may record partial payments; the remaining balance remains payable according to the applicable terms.

Payment confirmation is recorded separately from bank reconciliation. A pending, failed or disputed transaction is not reported as final reconciled payment. Changed beneficiary details must follow secure verification; an email alone is not sufficient authorisation to change the payout destination. Approved supplier tax and any lawful deductions are recorded separately and not treated as a change to the commission rate.

10. Refunds, reversals and recovery

If an eligible provider commission is legitimately reversed, refunded or clawed back, the corresponding Introducer share may be adjusted under the agreed rules and evidence. The adjustment refers to the original entry and uses its original rate and currency. The original settled record is not overwritten.

The treatment of tax credits, actual provider refunds and recovery from the Introducer is recorded separately. An agreed offset against unbilled future commission or a documented refund may be used where permitted by the applicable terms and obligations. The relevant scope, evidence, dispute process and notice requirements are [to be completed]. A debit must not automatically be treated as actual cash recovered.

11. Suspension, termination and accrued obligations

The referral link may be suspended separately from the account's access to existing financial obligations. Grounds, notice and any immediate-suspension exceptions are [to be agreed]. Termination notice is [period and method].

Suspension or termination stops new referrals within its agreed scope but does not silently remove earned, undisputed commission, pending invoices or required historical evidence. The Introducer retains a restricted settlement/document process for existing obligations, subject to justified security/compliance restrictions.

Post-termination commission entitlement for previously attributed relationships is [tail period / ongoing entitlement / qualifying cutoff / exclusions to be expressly agreed]. The treatment of a later provider receipt following termination must be defined here before production; it is not decided solely by a status flag.

12. Amendments and records

Commercial changes require an accepted replacement Schedule 1 or signed Addendum with an effective date, except where the executed terms explicitly authorise a particular prospective change. The relevant signed/accepted versions and evidence remain available in My Documents. A change to an internal Admin setting is not, by itself, a contractual amendment.

Historical commission entries retain their original rate and contract references. Corrections and adjustments are appended with reason/evidence rather than silently editing settled history.

13. Notices, dispute resolution and general terms

Operational statement notices may be sent to the approved account email without client names or sensitive financial amounts, directing the Introducer to sign in. Formal contractual notices follow [addresses, channel and delivery rules].

Governing law: [jurisdiction]. Dispute forum / procedure: [to be agreed]. Liability, exclusions, remedies, anti-bribery/financial-crime obligations, assignment, entire agreement, severability and force majeure terms: [appropriate reviewed provisions to be completed]. The commercial draft must not be executed with these provisions left unresolved.

Signature and acceptance record

For SOPHIMA: [name, capacity, signature, date/time].

For the Introducer: [name, capacity, authority, signature, date/time].

Platform evidence: [document hash], [agreement/schedule version], [verified signatory account], [signature/acceptance method], [audit event ID].

B. Schedule 1 — Standard Automatic Tier Commercial Terms

Agreement reference: [Master Agreement version/hash]. Introducer: [name/ID]. Effective: [UTC date/time]. Schedule version: [version].

1. Standard percentages

Distinct qualified successful clientsApplicable share of eligible Net Provider Commission
One30%
Two40%
Three or more50%

Before the first qualifying success, the starting contractual rate is 30%, pending successful qualification and actual eligible provider receipts. A starting rate is not commission earned merely by submitting an application.

2. Qualification

A qualified successful client is one distinct, verified canonical client for whom at least one qualifying provider relationship has successfully opened or activated with supporting provider evidence. The same client with several accounts, providers, products or applications counts once. Renaming an existing entity or changing its contact email does not create a new successful client. Distinct legal entities are separate clients after identity verification.

For financing, the qualification event is the first verified disbursement (approved prototype rule C03). Submission, matching, introduction, provider pre-approval or an indicative financing offer is not by itself qualification.

3. Prospective portfolio effect

The highest achieved automatic tier is retained. When the second or third distinct client qualifies, the higher rate applies prospectively across all eligible provider commission entries in that Introducer's portfolio under the agreed entitlement timestamp. A closure, decline of another application or lower activity does not automatically downgrade it.

Previously posted, invoiced, paid or reconciled entries retain their original rates. Reversals use the rate of the original entry. Qualification timestamp and effective commercial event are retained; the platform must not count one client several times.

4. Net, invoices and payments

The eligible net definition, entitlement timestamp, portal invoicing, currency separation, reviewed invoice tax, payment terms and recovery conditions in the Master Agreement apply. Share = approved eligible net amount × applicable rate, rounded to the currency's minor-unit precision once per final entry. Partial reversals use cumulative rounding so a complete reversal equals the original share.

Provider scope / exclusions: [if any]. Payment period: [agreed trigger and days]. Claim/dispute process: [reference]. Termination tail: [reference to completed Master clause].

5. Contractual amendment

A prospective reduction or bespoke rate requires the contractual authority specified in the executed Master Agreement and an accepted amendment or replacement Schedule where required. Such changes are visible as the applicable commercial terms to the Introducer; internal Admin reasons and controls remain confidential.

Accepted for SOPHIMA: [signatory/date]. Accepted for Introducer: [signatory/date]. Document hash and audit reference: [values].

C. Schedule 1 — Bespoke Commercial Terms (manual rate)

Agreement reference: [Master Agreement version/hash]. Introducer: [name/ID]. Effective: [UTC date/time]. Schedule version: [version]. Replaces: [prior Schedule/version or none].

1. Agreed commercial percentage

The applicable share is [agreed percentage]% of eligible Net Provider Commission for [entire attributed portfolio / precisely specified relationship scope]. The fictitious prototype demonstrates 35%; it is not a proposed or binding rate for a real introducer.

This agreed rate applies prospectively under the agreed entitlement timestamp until [expiry / replacement schedule / termination event]. It does not retrospectively change posted, invoiced, paid or reconciled entries.

2. Relationship to automatic tiers

Choose and complete one contractual option:

  • Bespoke rate while effective; automatic tiers resume on replacement: distinct-client automatic achievements continue to be tracked internally. When the accepted replacement Schedule switches back to the standard arrangement, the highest achieved standard tier applies prospectively.
  • Bespoke arrangement only: the standard automatic model does not govern this Introducer during [defined period/scope]. Any later return to automatic tiers requires a new agreed Schedule defining the resumed rate and qualification basis.

Do not leave both options operative. The Admin's “Manual Override” label and internal note are not part of the introducer-facing contract; the agreed percentage, scope and effective terms must be clear to both parties.

D. Addendum / replacement Schedule implementation checklist

The programmer must provide templates from approved contract content, not assemble a contract from an Admin percentage alone. On publishing a replacement: retain the prior signed document; show pending/new effective document separately; obtain required signatures/acceptance; record UTC effective timestamp; require a consistent resolved rate event; activate future terms at their effective time; preserve all original entry snapshots; and enable download of current and historical introducer-safe versions. Contract retention and access follow the approved legal/data schedule.

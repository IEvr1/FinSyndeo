Platform screening controls

Production screening sources: UN, EU, US OFAC and UK sanctions sources. FATF increased-monitoring and high-risk classifications are assessed separately. Additional regimes, including Ukraine, require an explicit applicable-policy configuration.

Applications subject to an applicable legal prohibition must not be released for introduction. Your acceptance selections cannot override a legal prohibition. Please record your institution’s additional commercial or risk restrictions here.

UBO nationality and residence remain available for truthful disclosure and are checked alongside identity and ownership/control information. Country availability does not confirm eligibility.

This review page does not check live lists or block applications. The production implementation must record source versions, update health and screening decisions, and hold release if required checks cannot be completed.
